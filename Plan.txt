KINGDOM SYSTEM
> Each player will only choose ONE kingdom to join.
> if the player refuses to join any kingdom, they will be known as "Wanderers"

KINGDOM SPECIALTIES(Changable)
Chronvara: Redstone & Automation - Clocks, farms, mechanisms, time-based contraptions, technical builds
Noctyra: PvP / Combat - Duels, weapons, combat training, bounty hunts, battle events
Verdentia: Farming & Livestock - Crops, food production, breeding, animal husbandry, sustainable living
Astraelis: Exploration & Enchanting - Nether/End exploration, rare resources, enchantments, celestial-themed builds

ROLES
> will grind materials according to their roles picked. Once done, they can sell their materials to the appropriate price range. For labor type roles (builder, PvP, Redstone, etc), they will be paid for their work.
